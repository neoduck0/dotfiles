Give your opinion on the following:

${@:-The topic or proposal we are currently discussing.}

- Lead with your verdict and the main reason for it.
- Challenge weak assumptions rather than agreeing by default.
- Cover the important trade-offs, risks, and alternatives.
- Explain what would change your recommendation.
- Distinguish facts from judgments and state relevant uncertainty.
- If the subject is unclear, ask a clarifying question.
- This is a discussion, not an implementation request.
- Be concise, a few short paragraphs unless the topic demands more.
